About

A planning studio, not a chatbot.

AI Home Renovation Planner turns a room description, goals, budget, and preferences into a structured renovation plan — built by specialized AI agents working together, then reviewed by an AI critic before it reaches you.

What the platform does

It combines an AI interior design assistant, a renovation cost planner, material intelligence, a shopping planner, and an execution assistant into one workspace — so you can go from "I want to renovate my bedroom" to a complete, actionable plan without hiring anyone first.

How AI renovation planning works

Your answers from the planner become a renovation brief. An orchestrator passes only the relevant parts of that brief to specialized agents — design concept, space planning, materials, budget, shopping list, timeline, and execution — each returning structured, validated output. A critic agent then reviews the combined plan for budget conflicts, timeline conflicts, and missing steps before it's finalized.

How budget planning works

The budget agent allocates your target amount across materials, furniture, labor, lighting, decor, and contingency, and flags a budget risk level. These are planning estimates, not contractor quotations — actual costs vary by location, labor, materials, and availability.

How material alternatives work

The material library and material agent compare options by cost level, maintenance, durability, and style fit, and explain trade-offs rather than declaring one material universally best.

How the execution plan is created

The execution agent sequences tasks into before-renovation, during-renovation, installation, styling, and final-inspection stages, noting dependencies and where a qualified professional is recommended — especially for electrical, gas, structural, or major plumbing work.

What this platform is not

It does not produce architectural drawings, engineering plans, construction approvals, or professional inspections, and it does not guarantee costs or timelines. For structural, electrical, gas, or major plumbing work, always consult a qualified professional.
